# Roll command-center visual reference

This is the approved desktop visual north star for the Roll rating redesign.

<img src="./roll-command-center-reference.jpg" alt="ComicPile Roll command-center desktop reference showing The Comic, Reading Context, and Your Context pillars" width="1200" />

## What this reference locks

- Three top-aligned console pillars: **01 The Comic**, **02 Reading Context**, **03 Your Context**.
- The Comic is cover-led and editorial.
- Reading Context is the widest pillar and makes the local reading chain a major visual element.
- Your Context makes the current rating, die consequence, personal analytics, and completion actions dominant.
- The visual language is near-black/ink with semantic amber, cyan/teal, and violet accents rather than a brown-dominant theme.
- `Mark Read & Save` is the decisive action; Snooze and Cancel are intentionally paired secondary actions.
- The desktop composition is a cockpit, not a loose generic card grid.

## Interpretation notes

The cover is intentionally a placeholder. Comic names, ratings, dates, creators, crossover names, and other sample values are illustrative only. Implementations should reproduce the **composition, hierarchy, density, spacing, and semantic visual roles**, then bind them to real ComicPile data.

Mobile is not represented by this image. The responsive contract in the implementation issue remains authoritative for tablet and mobile behavior.
