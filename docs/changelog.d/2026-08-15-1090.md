### Reading route explanation

- [#1090](https://github.com/JoshCLWren/comic-pile/pull/1090) adds an expanded dependency and reading-route explanation to the Roll screen, reachable from each route summary. The modal shows continuity eligibility, satisfied prerequisites, unresolved blockers, bounded transitive chains with convergent lanes, verified downstream unlocks, hard prerequisite threads, and named route memberships. Informational route membership is clearly separated from hard dependencies. Opening and closing preserves the pending roll, unsaved rating, die preview, and scroll/focus state. Keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, and touch navigation are supported.
